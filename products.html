Our Products

Made for the center of the table.

Three creamy Mediterranean dips inspired by the flavors and spirit of Greece — made for sharing, gathering and turning everyday moments into something a little more beautiful.

The Original

Creamy Feta Dip

Rich, tangy feta meets creamy Greek yogurt, roasted red pepper, lemon, garlic and Mediterranean herbs in a smooth, savory dip made for gathering around the table.

Ingredients

Sheep's Milk Feta Cheese, Greek Yogurt, Cream Cheese, Roasted Red Pepper, Olive Oil, Lemon Juice, Garlic, Oregano, Crushed Red Pepper.

Contains: Milk

Net Wt. 10 oz (283 g)

Serving Inspiration

Serve with warm pita, crisp vegetables or crackers; spread onto sandwiches and wraps; or place it at the center of a Mediterranean grazing board.

EASY IDEA: SANTORINI MEZZE BOARD

Spoon the dip into a shallow serving bowl and surround it with warm pita, cucumbers, tomatoes, olives and your favorite Mediterranean accompaniments.

Bold & Vibrant

Roasted Red Pepper Creamy Feta Dip

Creamy feta and Greek yogurt meet roasted red pepper, garlic, lemon and Mediterranean herbs for a colorful dip with a rich, savory character.

Ingredients

Sheep's Milk Feta Cheese, Greek Yogurt, Cream Cheese, Roasted Red Pepper, Garlic, Lemon Juice, Olive Oil, Oregano, Crushed Red Pepper.

Contains: Milk

Net Wt. 10 oz (283 g)

Serving Inspiration

Pair with toasted pita or fresh vegetables, spoon alongside grilled foods, spread onto a sandwich or add it to a Mediterranean-style platter.

EASY IDEA: ROASTED VEGETABLE PITA

Spread generously onto warm pita and top with roasted vegetables and fresh herbs for a simple Mediterranean-inspired lunch or appetizer.

Mediterranean Classic

Kalamata Olive Creamy Feta Dip

Savory olives join creamy feta, Greek yogurt, roasted red pepper, lemon, garlic and herbs for a distinctly Mediterranean flavor inspired by the tables of Greece.

Ingredients

Sheep's Milk Feta Cheese, Greek Yogurt, Cream Cheese, Roasted Red Pepper, Garlic, Lemon Juice, Olive Oil, Olives, Oregano, Crushed Red Pepper.

Contains: Milk

Net Wt. 10 oz (283 g)

Serving Inspiration

Pair with pita and vegetables, add to a mezze board, spread onto crostini or serve alongside your favorite Mediterranean dishes.

EASY IDEA: KALAMATA CROSTINI

Spread onto toasted slices of baguette and finish with fresh tomato, herbs or a light drizzle of olive oil for an easy shared appetizer.
